PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 9 July 2026

We're two founders who are also users of this app. We built GETUPP because we wanted it, not to build an ad network. This policy is written to be readable by humans, not only lawyers. If something is unclear, email us: hello@getupp.co.

1. WHAT GETUPP DOES

GETUPP is an iOS app that uses Apple's Screen Time and Family Controls technology to temporarily block the apps you choose during a morning window you define. Each morning, those apps remain blocked until you take a live photo confirming you are out of bed. Once the photo is verified, your apps are unlocked for the day.

The app is a digital wellbeing tool. It is not a parental-control product; it is designed for adults who want to change their own morning habits. You are always in control of which apps are blocked and during which time window.

2. SCREEN TIME & APP-BLOCKING DATA

GETUPP uses Apple's Family Controls framework to apply and remove app restrictions. All Screen Time data — including which apps are blocked and for how long — is processed entirely on your device. This data never leaves your phone and is never sent to our servers.

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any Screen Time or app-usage information. Apple's framework enforces this at the OS level.

  • We cannot see which apps you block.
  • We cannot see your app-usage history.
  • We cannot access Screen Time data outside the scope of your session on your device.

3. YOUR VERIFICATION PHOTO

Each morning, GETUPP asks you to take a live photo to confirm you are out of bed. Here is exactly what happens to that photo:

  • Your photo is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS) to our AI verification service, which is powered by Anthropic's API.
  • The AI analyzes the photo solely to determine whether you appear to be out of bed.
  • The photo is deleted immediately after analysis. We never store it.
  • No human at GETUPP ever sees your photo.
  • We keep only the result (pass or fail), the timestamp, and your streak count.
About Anthropic

Anthropic acts as a data processor on our behalf for photo verification. Anthropic does not use data sent via their API to train their models. For Anthropic's current API data usage policy, visit anthropic.com/privacy.

4. ACCOUNT & EMAIL

If you join our waitlist, we collect your email address. We use it only to notify you when GETUPP is available on the App Store and to send occasional pre-launch updates. We do not sell your email address. We do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

You can unsubscribe from waitlist emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email we send. After launch, account deletion will remove your email and all associated data from our servers.

We use Supabase to store waitlist emails. Supabase is a US-based cloud infrastructure provider. For more information, see supabase.com/privacy.

5. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

  • We do not sell your data. Ever.
  • We do not serve ads.
  • We do not share personal data with third parties except as described above (Anthropic for photo verification; Supabase for email storage).
  • We do not use tracking pixels or third-party analytics SDKs in the app.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, microphone, or camera roll — only the live photo you explicitly take within the app.

6. CHILDREN

GETUPP is intended for users aged 17 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify waitlist subscribers by email and update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
